Why these effects and not a generic byte hexer

In these files every 32-bit word is one pixel byte plus three audio bytes. A generic corruptor hits all four equally, which drowns the picture in noise and the sound in clipping at the same time. Here each effect declares which plane it touches — picture, sound or alignment — so you can wreck the image while the music stays clean, bend the sound while the video plays straight, or knock the row and frame stride sideways to get the rolling, tearing look the format is prone to anyway.
